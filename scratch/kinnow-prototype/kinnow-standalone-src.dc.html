Four judgements, before the peel.
Weight in the hand first. Then colour, then the button, then how the skin answers a thumb.
02 — THE PEEL
How it releases tells us plenty.
A good Kinnow lets its rind away cleanly, in one piece, leaving the flesh dry and the fingers oiled.
03 — WHAT IS INSIDE
Ten segments, held together.
They should part willingly and hold their shape. Segments that collapse have been picked too late.
04 — THE CROSS SECTION
Cut across, it cannot hide anything.
Ten wedges packed to the rind, a small tight core, juice standing at the face. This is the test that settles a crate.
05 — THE SELECTION TEST
Colour is the easiest thing to admire. Weight is the harder truth.
Three sound fruits. One is heavy for its size, tight at the button and even in colour — that is the one we send.
